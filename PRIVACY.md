# Page Redline — Privacy Policy

Page Redline stores the edits and notes you make (the marked text, your replacement text, the
reason and note you attach, and the URL and title of the page they belong to) together with
your settings (reasons, colors, visibility) in your browser's extension storage, on your device
only.

The extension makes no network requests. It does not send, sync or share any data. Nothing
leaves your browser unless you use **Export**, which writes a JSON file to a location you choose.

The extension requests access to all websites so that its context-menu entries and keyboard
shortcuts can mark text on any page you are reading. It reads a page's text only to place and
re-find your marks on that page.

Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.
